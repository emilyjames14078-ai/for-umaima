Happy Birthday, My Emily ❤️

To Umaima, My Emily,

First of all, happy birthday. ❤️ I pray that Allah fills your life with happiness, peace, good health, and countless beautiful moments. May He protect your smile, make your dreams come true, and guide you toward everything that is good for you.

On your special day, I just want you to know that you are someone important to me. No matter how complicated things have sometimes become between us, I still genuinely wish the best for you.

We may have had misunderstandings, made mistakes, or struggled to understand each other at times. But I believe that every experience teaches us something. I have learned that caring for someone also means being patient, listening, understanding, and respecting their feelings.

I don't want this letter to put any pressure on you or make you feel that you owe me anything. I simply wanted to make your birthday a little more special and share a few sincere words with you.

May Allah always keep you safe, bless your family, bring peace to your heart, and give you success in everything you work toward. May the coming year bring you more reasons to smile than ever before.

Thank you for being a part of my story and for the moments that have meant something to me. Whatever life brings, I hope you always remember your worth and never stop believing in the beautiful things that are still ahead of you.

Happy birthday once again, Umaima. May your day be as special as the happiness you deserve. ❤️

With sincere wishes and duas,

Abdullah With care, always ❤️
